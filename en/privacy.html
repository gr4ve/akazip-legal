AkaZip Privacy Policy

Bonboludus Studio · Effective 11 October 2026

Scope

This policy explains how AkaZip, provided by Bonboludus Studio, handles data on macOS and iOS. AkaZip is a local tool for browsing archives, previewing content and extracting files. You do not need to register an AkaZip account.

Files and local processing

The app reads only archives you choose to open, split volumes you explicitly select and directories you authorize. It writes extracted files to the destination you select. File contents, filenames, paths, archive metadata and archive passwords are not uploaded to Bonboludus Studio servers. The app contains no advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDK and does not track you across apps.

File providers

If you choose files or destinations in iCloud Drive or another file provider, the system and that provider may download, upload or synchronize those files. Their handling is governed by that service’s privacy policy and your settings. AkaZip does not separately send your files to the operator.

Temporary files and passwords

Content previews, nested archive browsing and coordination with file providers may create temporary local copies. The app cleans up these copies when the relevant resources are closed or released and limits their storage through local cache budgets. After an abnormal exit, system temporary-file mechanisms may also perform cleanup. Files you deliberately export remain in the selected directory. Archive passwords are used only for the current archive task and are not written to preferences or logs.

Local preferences

The app stores file-access bookmarks for your selected extraction directory, file-association preferences and onboarding completion on your device. These are used to restore directory selections and interface preferences and are not sent to the operator. macOS maintains default file associations. Uninstalling the app may not automatically remove system file associations or files you have exported.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple’s App Store and StoreKit handle Pro payments, purchase records, purchase restoration and subscription management. AkaZip receives verified product identifiers, purchase times, expiration times and valid entitlement status to determine Pro access. The operator does not receive payment-card details, your Apple account password or archive contents. See the Apple Privacy Policy for how Apple handles purchase and device data.

Contacting support

If you choose to send feedback to bonboludus@gmail.com, we use the email address, problem description and attachments you voluntarily provide to respond and investigate. Please avoid sending archive passwords or unrelated sensitive files. You may request deletion of relevant support information through the same email address. Support information is kept only as long as needed to handle your request and meet applicable obligations.

Your choices

You can stop using the app, close previews, delete exported files or change authorized directories through the system file-selection process. Cancel Pro renewal through Apple’s subscription management; deleting the app does not automatically cancel a subscription. The operator cannot remotely read or delete local archives on your device.

Policy website hosting

This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. These website access records are separate from AkaZip’s local archive processing. The app does not upload archive contents to the website hosting service as a result.

Policy updates and contact

If our data-handling practices change, we will update this policy and its version date and obtain relevant authorization when required. Privacy contact: Bonboludus Studio; email: bonboludus@gmail.com.
